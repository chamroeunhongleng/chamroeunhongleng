Product Work

Chomkar OrderLoop

Field-validated 2026 · Agritech market coordination

ML roadmap, business analysis, and field validation

  • Conducted field research with the team across ~30 bok choy farmers in Kang Meas, Kampong Cham, to test whether pre-harvest demand visibility is the real constraint for smallholders.
  • Scoped a buyer-first order loop instead of a broad marketplace, using a cooperator as the operational aggregation point rather than assuming every farmer manages a complex app.
  • Concept placed Top 2 at TSC Turing Hackathon Cycle 10, Market Access for Farmers (team result).
  • Bounded the pilot thesis to one recurring buyer, one cooperator, and 30–100 farmers, with fulfillment rate, coordination time, spoilage, and payment timing as the metrics that decide what gets built next. Unit economics and retention are documented as hypotheses, not results.

Methods  Field interviews · product strategy · workflow design · unit-economics framing

Chomkar Decision Grid

Released 2026 · Decision intelligence · open source

Framework design, deterministic engine, and AI workflow

  • Turned a commercial problem — a buyer needs 1–3 tonnes while individual farms offer a few hundred kilograms — into an auditable engine that assembles farmer lots with transparent prices, allocations, and route risk.
  • Modelled the workflow as Detect → Evaluate → Recommend → Act with an independent audit gate that re-derives payouts, buyer caps, and the winning score, so pricing and fairness can be defended to both sides of a deal.
  • Backed by 62 unit tests and CI, with designed failure cases so “cannot fulfill” is a valid answer; bilingual Khmer/English reports close with a human-approval checklist. The framework recommends — it does not authorize purchases, payments, or deliveries.

Stack  Python · unit tests · CI · decision audit · bilingual reporting
